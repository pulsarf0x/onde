Keep your Android phone and listen on your AirPlay speakers.

Onde sends playback audio from Android apps that allow audio capture to compatible receivers on your local Wi-Fi network. Select a receiver, approve Android’s capture prompt and start playback in your usual music app.

Features include local AirPlay discovery, speaker volume controls, a capture level meter, a stop notification, a sleep timer, player shortcuts, optional track information and user-initiated diagnostic sharing.

Your phone and receiver must share a local network. A HomePod must already be configured with the required Apple devices and allow your AirPlay connection.

Compatibility varies by phone, Android version, music app and receiver. Some apps or protected content block audio capture; Onde does not bypass these restrictions. Audio latency is expected. Synchronization with video or games and screen-off playback are not guaranteed.

Onde has no account, ads or analytics SDK. Audio is streamed to the local receivers you choose, not recorded by the app or sent to the developer’s server. Android’s playback-capture permission is required. Onde does not open the physical microphone or transmit screen images. Notification access for track metadata is optional.
